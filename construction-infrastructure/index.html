Sectors

Construction & Infrastructure

Contracts, project delivery and disputes involving construction and infrastructure works.

Construction projects bring together owners, contractors, consultants and suppliers with different obligations and commercial priorities. Legal support should clarify those obligations, allocate risk and provide a practical route for addressing difficulties during performance.

How we can assist

  • Drafting and review of construction, consultancy and subcontracting agreements
  • Advice on payment obligations, variations, delay provisions and contractual notices
  • Assessment of contractual liability, defects and termination issues
  • Negotiation and resolution of construction-related disputes

Issues that may arise

The situations below are illustrative. They are not a statement of past client matters.

  • Disagreement over additional works or outstanding payments
  • Delayed delivery, alleged defects or disputed acceptance of works
  • Termination of a contractor or subcontractor agreement

How we work

We begin with the facts, the relevant contracts and the client’s objectives. We identify the legal questions, explain the available options and agree a practical course of action. The precise scope of any engagement is confirmed with the client.
